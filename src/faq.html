Hi! Here's some Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs), and their answers, of course!

How does this service work?

This service functions by using a completely stateless architecture, where a challenge token is generated using free compute server-side, passed to you to clearsign, and then verified.

The real magic is the fact that the token is a JWT made using server-side secrets, because the JWT is itself signed internally, the service can know it did in fact issue that token! This, alongside the fact that we require you clearsign, rather than detached sign, means you hand the service back its own issued JWT once you sign it, so the service doesn't need to store anything!

How is this service hosted?

This service is hosted entirely on a free-tier Cloudflare worker, which is the reason it was made to be totally stateless!

How was this service made?

This service was made entirely by ItsThatOneJack (ITOJ), with small bits of help from coding-adept Large Language Models such as ChatGPT and Claude Sonnet, the LLMs were involved in the process of ensuring spec compliance, and implementation of the page rendering system.

Is any of my data shared?

No, none of your data, other than what is required to perform OpenID Connect's flow, is ever shared with another party or even stored on this service!

This service is completely stateless, and so is not capable of storing your data apart from in memory during token generation and verification.

However, Cloudflare, which this service is hosted on, may record data such as your device fingerprint, which is why you may have seen your ad/tracker blocker find and block a single tracker.

Why must I sometimes provide extra info?

OpenID Connect is fundamentally a system that exists solely to convey your identity to other services.
Because of this, sometimes services request more info, like a preferred username, or your email. In these cases, ITOJ OIDC will ask you for these details before authenticating you with the RP (Relying Party) service.
